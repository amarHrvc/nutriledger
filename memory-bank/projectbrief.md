# Project Brief

**Last Updated:** 2025-12-24

## Overview
Nutri Ledger is a Laravel-based clinical nutrition record system for tracking patients, visits, vitals, labs, labs, medications, recommendations, and reminders.

## Goals
- Provide a focused MVP for small clinics and nutrition practices to manage patients and their encounters.
- Make daily workflows (register patient, add visit, record labs/vitals, manage medications and recommendations, schedule reminders) fast and reliable.
- Establish a solid, testable foundation (roles, schema, seeders, factories) that can be safely extended later.

## Scope
- In scope:
  - Core user roles (admin, doctor, patient) and role-based access control.
  - Patient registration and socio-economic data capture.
  - Visit and encounter tracking with vitals, labs, medications, recommendations, and reminders.
  - Basic dashboards and summaries for clinic and patient overviews.
- Out of scope:
  - Complex billing, insurance, or inventory modules.
  - Full-scale EMR features (e.g. imaging or e-prescription integrations).
  - Native mobile apps or non-web clients for this MVP.
